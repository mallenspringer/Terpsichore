First copper wire,
then steady static hums—
one breath between lines.

Count every stressed beat:
ta-dum, ta-dum, silver bell—
hold long at the break.

Cold glass holds the light;
warm hands trace the dusty pane;
silence clears the frame.

Speak softly and fast,
chasing ripples across stone
while echoes catch up.

Pause here...
wait until tension gathers,
then finish the stanza clean.

Last signal fading—
wrap neatly back to card one
and begin again.
